Two good lists came before this one and both went quiet — [swyxio/devtools-angels](https://github.com/swyxio/devtools-angels) (archived, and now pointing here) and [Nutlope/aiangels](https://github.com/Nutlope/aiangels) (inactive since 2024). This one picks up that work as an independent compilation: every entry is checked against public sources and carries a last-verified date, so you can tell fresh data from stale.

If you came from devtools-angels, most of the names you knew aren't here. That was a 2021-era devtools snapshot; this list requires an AI focus and public evidence of a check inside the last 18 months. A few of those names were re-researched and left off for lack of current evidence, but most simply haven't been worked yet. If you're one of them and still investing, [add yourself](#add-an-angel) — it's one row and a source link.

**For founders:** find angels who write checks in your space, with check sizes and how to reach them.

**For angels:** [add yourself](#add-an-angel) with a PR.

## Add an angel

Open a PR that adds one row to the table, with a source link for the activity claim. Adding yourself is welcome and encouraged — say so in the PR. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the field format.

## Inclusion criteria

An entry stays on the list when all of these hold:

1. **Angel checks, personally directed** — personal capital, or a rolling fund/syndicate the person leads. Fund partners who only deploy fund capital belong in a VC directory, not here.
2. **AI focus** — infra, devtools, agents, models, or applied AI as a stated or demonstrated focus area.
3. **Active** — public evidence of investing within the last 18 months: an announced check, a founder's thank-you, press coverage, or a current "actively investing" statement from the person themselves.
4. **Verifiable** — every entry links the public source that backs it. No source, no entry.

Contact details are limited to channels the person publishes for pitches (their own site, a public form, an X handle). We never list scraped emails.

## Removal and staleness

- Anyone listed can opt out: open an issue or email the maintainer, and the entry comes down, no questions asked.
- Entries whose last-verified date passes 18 months get re-checked; if no fresh activity turns up, they move to a dormant section rather than silently rotting.

## Maintenance

Reviewed weekly. PRs meeting the criteria merge fast.
